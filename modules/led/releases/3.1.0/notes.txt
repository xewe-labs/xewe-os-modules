led 3.1.0

Drives an addressable LED strip with FastLED: 46 chipsets, seven effects with a cross-fade between
them, software brightness and colour order, a first-boot wizard and 30 $led commands. Other modules
follow every LED change through a listener. This is the module's first release; it carries the
LED engine of xewe-led-os 2.3.0 over to XeWe OS, with the same command names, arguments and texts,
so button and schedule commands stored on devices keep working.

Changed compared with xewe-led-os 2.3.0

- Smaller and faster: the status and parameter texts no longer use std::stringstream, which takes
  the whole iostream runtime out of the image (about 225-250 KB less flash and 4.6-4.8 KB less RAM
  for the module alone on c3, c6 and s3). Mode parameters are constant tables, and the two noise
  effects look their parameters up once per frame instead of once per pixel. The text output is
  unchanged.
- Re-running the colour-order calibration ($led set_color_order "") on a strip that is already
  calibrated now finds the right order. Before, a GRB strip came out as RGB the second time.
- $led set voltage refuses any value other than 5, 12 or 24 before it stores or echoes anything.
- A hue or saturation taken from a colour now stays inside the mode's range: Color Fade's Min
  Saturation stops at 245, where 2.3.0 stored 246-255.
- The calibration and saturation items change behaviour compared with 2.3.0; the voltage item fixes
  a bug of the port. Command names, arguments and texts are unchanged.
- The board test sends each command only after the board has echoed the previous one, so the
  core's input queue (4 lines) never overflows during the round-trip tests.

Requirements

- XeWeCore >=3.1.0,<4.0.0 (requires_core). ESP32-C3, C6 or S3 with arduino-esp32 3.x.
- Library: FastLED 3.10.3 (pinned in libraries.toml). Modules: none.

Not verified by the release card

Strip-only and visual checks (colours on a real strip, effect timing, the wizard on a fresh board)
are done by hand; the card lists what its board session ran.
