led-web 3.1.2

The LED control page in the browser. 3.1.2 removes the module's two defines; the page, the routes
and the frames behave as in 3.1.1.

Changed

- XEWE_LED_WEB_WS_PORT and XEWE_LED_WEB_HEARTBEAT_MS are gone. The port 81 and the one-second
  heartbeat are fixed constants in LedWeb.h, matched to the page script. The module has no
  configurable values; both sections of its Config.h are empty.

Breaking

- A project that defined either name gets an unused define and no effect.

Requirements

- XeWeCore >=3.1.0,<4.0.0 (requires_core); the led and web-interface modules as in 3.1.1.

Testing

What was tested, on which tree and with which result: release_card.md, generated by
scripts/release.sh --prepare 3.1.2 led led-web and attached to this release.
